# Landing surface brief

## Scope and mode
Landing page at `/`; mode: Persuade.

## Audience and job
Primary audience: owner or operations leader responsible for a customer-onboarding team. The visitor should understand Flowboard's mechanism and start a workspace.

## Action
Primary CTA: Start free. Returning users can open the workspace; sign-in remains secondary.

## Proof and content
Use true product mechanisms: workflow stages, customer cards, required checklists, staff reminders, WhatsApp actions, and human handover. Synthetic interface records are allowed when clearly illustrative. Do not invent testimonials, customer logos, benchmarks, or proof assets.

## Constraints
English landing copy. Preserve responsive web behavior, keyboard access, focus, reduced motion, and a global light/dark toggle. Keep app routes and authenticated surfaces untouched.

## Direction
Shared Flowboard workspace language: white canvas, indigo actions, slate lanes, rounded cards, soft shadows, Plus Jakarta Sans, and explicit queued/progress/urgent/done states. The landing should feel like the same product as the dashboard and login flow, not a separate marketing brand.

## Memorable moment
Trace handover turns a customer reply into a visible staff action in the hero ledger.

## Unresolved
Pricing, social proof, and external WhatsApp provider details remain outside this landing build until product decisions are confirmed.
